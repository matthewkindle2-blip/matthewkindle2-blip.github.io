Ultimate training · Enterprise qualification

MEDDPICC Third-Person Deal Lab

No MEDDPICC experience required. After the Dream Squad curriculum, step into the qualifier — a third-person 3D deal floor where you learn enterprise qualification from zero. Each room uses plain-English discovery questions, explains the eight scorecard checks as you play, and ramps from Level 1 (Northwind Logistics) through disqualifying fake deals, paper process, and expansion.

  • Lock controls first — prevents page scroll while you play; press Esc or Unlock to exit
  • Walk the lab — WASD + mouse (desktop) or joystick + drag-to-look (phone)
  • New to MEDDPICC? — open the Cortex briefing first; questions and hints teach each letter as you play
  • Discovery questions — plain language you can actually say on a call; trap moves show what not to do
  • Forecast for real — Commit, Best Case, Pipeline, or Disqualify with coaching on each outcome

How to play — read before you enter

Step 1 · Enter the lab

Tap Enter the Deal Lab inside the simulator. Walk to glowing portals — the MEDDPICC Cortex first, then five account rooms.

Step 2 · Lock controls

Tap Lock controls before you move. This stops the page from scrolling while you play. On desktop, locking also captures your mouse so you can look around.

Step 3 · Unlock anytime

Press Esc or tap Unlock controls to scroll the Academy page again. Deal progress saves automatically.

Desktop controls (after lock)

  • W / A / S / D — move
  • Mouse — look (after click-to-lock)
  • Shift — sprint
  • E — enter portal when prompted

Inside each deal room

  • Read the Level badge — start at Northwind (Level 1) if MEDDPICC is new
  • Pick discovery questions — each costs one effort point; hints explain what you are learning
  • Watch the scorecard light up (Metrics, Buyer, Pain, etc.)
  • Choose Commit · Best Case · Pipeline · Disqualify
  • Read manager coaching — replay rooms to level up
  • Jump to simulator
  • Dream Squad curriculum
