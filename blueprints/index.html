The California Revenue Roles Blueprint

Preview real blueprint sections — hubs, target company types, outreach angles, interview prep, and a 7-day action plan — before you subscribe.

Top hubs

  • Silicon Valley / Bay Area — San Francisco, Palo Alto, South Bay
  • Silicon Beach — Los Angeles, Santa Monica, Irvine
  • Biotech Coast — San Diego, La Jolla

5 target company types

  • Enterprise SaaS
  • AI/ML
  • cybersecurity
  • growth-stage unicorns
  • Creator economy

3 outreach angles

  • Follow Anthropic, Rippling, Figma — engage with their GTM content weekly.
  • Connect with Bay Area Council and LA Tech & Innovation Council members and San Francisco SDR/AE alumni — personalize every request.
  • Post weekly: one California market observation, one sales skill insight, one employer-specific comment.

5 interview questions to prep

  1. Why this revenue role, and why this company in this California hub?
  2. Walk me through a discovery call for a technical buyer in enterprise SaaS.
  3. How do you prioritize pipeline when 30+ accounts compete for your time?
  4. For Silicon Valley / Bay Area: how would you emphasize stack fluency, integration paths, and security posture?
  5. For Silicon Beach: how would you emphasize storytelling, cross-industry transferability, and brand-forward positioning?

7-day action plan (preview)

Day 1

Map all live California reqs on Aethara — filter by hub (Silicon Valley / Bay Area, Silicon Beach, Biotech Coast) and role category.

Day 2

Build CRM/spreadsheet: employer, hub, role, Hiring Signal, hiring manager LinkedIn, apply link.

Day 3

Set LinkedIn headline to target role + California hub focus (e.g., "SDR | San Francisco SaaS").

Day 4

Join Bay Area Council and LA Tech & Innovation Council newsletter and one hub-specific meetup group.

Day 5

Apply to top 5 Aggressive-tier reqs (Hiring Signal 80+).

Day 6

Send 15 targeted LinkedIn connection requests to SDR managers and AEs at anchor employers.

Day 7

Attend one Bay Area Council and LA Tech & Innovation Council event or virtual webinar — ask one substantive question publicly.
