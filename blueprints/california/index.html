🎯 Core Revenue Role Career Paths

Path 1: Sales (Outbound/Closing)

SDR/BDR → AE (Associate) → AE (Mid-Market) → AE (Enterprise) → Senior AE → Sales Manager → VP Sales → CRO
  • Timeline to $300K+: 4–7 years
  • SDR to AE promotion: 18–24 months at most companies

Path 2: Solution Engineering (Presales)

Entry-Level SE → Sales Engineer → Senior SE → Principal SE → SE Manager → Director SE
  • Entry-level SE salary: ~$82,247 (national benchmark)
  • Focus: Technical demos, POCs, architecture discussions

Path 3: Customer Success

CSM → Senior CSM → CSM Manager → Director CS → VP Customer Success
  • Expansion and renewal revenue ownership grows at senior levels

Path 4: Management

Team Lead (player-coach) → Manager → Director → VP → CRO
  • Player-coach roles common at Series B–D before full management track

🚀 Step-by-Step Entry Strategy

Step 1: Research & Specialization · 2–4 weeks

  • Choose a vertical aligned to San Francisco: SaaS, cybersecurity, FinTech, health-tech, or cloud
  • Map San Francisco, Los Angeles, San Diego employers with clear SDR→AE paths
  • Study product value propositions — use Aethara employer pages as anchor accounts
  • Key ecosystem: World's densest SaaS ecosystem - Salesforce, Adobe, Zoom, Workday, ServiceNow and hundreds of funded startups

Step 2: Skill Building · 4–8 weeks

  • Complete HubSpot Academy (free) + one paid course (LinkedIn Learning or Coursera)
  • Learn Salesforce or HubSpot CRM — most live reqs assume CRM fluency
  • Practice cold call scripts, discovery frameworks, and mock demos
  • Build vertical technical literacy (cloud, APIs, security, or data — match your hub)

Step 3: Resume & LinkedIn Optimization · 1 week

  • Quantify results: meetings booked, pipeline generated, quota attainment, B2B wins
  • Keywords: SDR, BDR, Account Executive, technical solutions, SaaS sales
  • Geo-tag San Francisco and enable "Open to Work" visible to recruiters
  • Featured section: one-pager or deck showing mock discovery or territory plan

Step 4: Network Strategically · ongoing

  • Attend Bay Area Council and LA Tech & Innovation Council events and regional revenue meetups
  • Connect with 5 SDR managers and 5 AEs at target companies on LinkedIn weekly
  • Join Pavilion, RevGenius, or Sales Hacker for async community visibility

Step 5: Apply for Entry Roles · 2–6 weeks

  • Target: SDR, BDR, Associate AE, Associate Sales Engineer, Technical Sales Rep
  • Aethara live anchors: Anthropic, Rippling, Block, Figma, OpenAI
  • Browse live California jobs ↓
  • Tailor each application — reference hub-specific buyer pain points

Step 6: Interview Preparation · 1–2 weeks

  • Three-question formula: 15-sec intro · Why revenue roles? · Why this company?
  • Prepare 2 STAR stories per hub you are targeting
  • Mock presentation: explain a product value prop in 3 minutes
  • Demonstrate research + coachability — top 3 traits managers want
